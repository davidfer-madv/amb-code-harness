### I.6 Partner delivery tenant isolation and secure destination handling

Requirement: every partner webhook endpoint, subscription, outbox event, retry and delivery is owned by exactly one brand; events are never delivered to, queried by, or replayed through another brand's endpoint, and the same isolation applies to the SSE firehose and journal-read APIs. Production destinations use HTTPS and are protected against SSRF, DNS rebinding and unsafe redirects. Apple attachment URLs, keys and signatures are not exposed in partner payloads (journal/log secret-minimization is broader; see I.3). MSP-platform security control; Apple's guide implies it through the opaque-ID privacy contract but does not specify the MSP→partner interface.
Reference: MSP Integration Guide §27 (Security and Privacy, 27.1 The Opaque ID), cross-ref I.1/B.8; Standard Webhooks specification. Verification: Provide Code (two-brand integration + adversarial SSRF test).

### H.3 Reliable, ordered, signed and revocable partner webhook delivery

Requirement: partner webhook events are created atomically with the business transaction and delivered under a documented at-least-once contract; each event carries a stable webhook-id, timestamp and verifiable Standard Webhooks signature; deliveries preserve per-conversation ordering, retry under a bounded policy, resist concurrent/stale workers via leases and fencing, support consumer dedup on webhook-id, and stop after endpoint deactivation. MSP-platform reliability control.
Reference: Cerebro Partner API (docs/partner-api.md); Standard Webhooks specification; analogous MSP Integration Guide §24, §8.3, §30.2. Verification: Provide Code (fault-injection delivery test).
