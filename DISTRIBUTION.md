# Distribution decision

## Recommended initial visibility: private

The reusable harness excludes raw candidate source, evidence, generated customer reports, packages,
large media and local credentials. Some retained historical prompts and OpenSpec design records use
named candidate case studies to preserve the reasoning behind adversarial checks. Those documents
may be appropriate for an authorized internal repository but require a separate content and rights
review before public distribution.

Before creating the GitHub remote, the owner must choose one of these paths:

1. **Private/internal repository:** retain the named case-study design history for authorized users.
2. **Public repository:** first generalize or remove named candidate/customer references, confirm
   rights to distribute historical checklist material, select a public license, and rerun
   `bin/github-readiness.py`.

No license is selected automatically. Absence of a license does not grant public reuse rights.
